Privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026. ClipForge is a local desktop application; this policy describes exactly what leaves your computer, and what does not.

1. We do not collect anything

ClipForge has no servers of its own and no analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or advertising. The developer receives no data about you, your videos or your uploads. There is no account to create and nothing to sign up for.

2. What stays on your computer

  • Imported footage, harvested livestream segments, transcripts, rendered clips and thumbnails.
  • The local database (data/clipforge.sqlite3) holding projects, clips, jobs and settings.
  • Google OAuth credentials — the client you configure, plus the access and refresh tokens issued when you connect a YouTube channel. These are stored only in that local database, are never displayed in full, and are never transmitted anywhere except to Google's own OAuth endpoints in order to refresh them.

3. What leaves your computer, and only when you ask

  • Video URLs you choose to clip. ClipForge resolves the URL you paste, so that host receives the request.
  • YouTube uploads. When you press upload for a clip, the rendered file, its title, description, tags and the visibility you selected are sent to Google's YouTube Data API v3 and published to the channel you chose. Nothing is uploaded without that explicit action.
  • First-run downloads. Subtitle fonts (from Google Fonts), the face detector (from the OpenCV project) and the Whisper speech model are downloaded once during installation. Your footage is never part of those requests.

4. Google user data

ClipForge requests two scopes: youtube.upload (to publish the clips you choose) and youtube.readonly (to show which channel is connected before you upload). ClipForge does not use Google user data for advertising, does not sell it, does not transfer it to third parties, and does not use it to train models. Access is limited to the channel(s) you connect.

You can revoke access at any time from ClipForge's Settings page (Remove on a channel, which also revokes the token with Google) or from myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing a channel deletes its tokens from the local database.

5. Retention and deletion

Everything ClipForge stores is a file on your disk. Deleting a project in the app removes its clips, transcript and working copy; deleting the application folder removes everything, including the database and any tokens. Nothing is retained by the developer because nothing was ever sent to the developer.

6. Children

ClipForge is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

7. Changes

Material changes to this policy will be committed to the public repository with an updated date at the top of this page.
